About this data: The NTSB investigates civil aviation accidents and incidents in the United States. This database includes accidents and selected incidents from 2008 to present. An accident involves death, serious injury, or substantial aircraft damage. Not all operators in this database have poor safety records — high-volume operators naturally appear more often. Use this data for awareness, not as a sole safety assessment.

Data source: NTSB Aviation Accident Database. Updated monthly. For full investigation reports, visit NTSB CAROL.
