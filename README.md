# Aviation Safety Check

Search NTSB aviation accident data by airline, charter operator, flight school, tail number, or aircraft type. 31,000+ civil aviation accidents and incidents since 2008 with probable cause narratives, fatality counts, and severity ratings.

**Live at:** https://greattombproductions.github.io/aviation-safety/

## Data Source

[NTSB Aviation Accident Database](https://data.ntsb.gov/avdata) — The National Transportation Safety Board investigates civil aviation accidents and selected incidents in the United States.

## Features

- **Search by Operator:** Find any airline, charter company, or flight school and see their full incident history — yearly trends, aircraft types, severity breakdown, probable causes
- **Search by Tail Number:** Look up an aircraft by FAA registration number to see its accident history
- **Search by Aircraft Type:** Compare accident records across aircraft makes and models
- **Incidents Near Me:** Enter an address and see aviation accidents within a configurable radius (10–50 miles)

## Important Note

An NTSB accident investigation doesn't necessarily mean an operator has poor safety practices. High-volume operators naturally appear more often. This tool is for awareness and research, not as a sole safety assessment.
